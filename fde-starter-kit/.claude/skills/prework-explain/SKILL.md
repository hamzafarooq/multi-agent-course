# /prework-explain <term or question>

The student asked about: `$ARGUMENTS`

1. Look the term up in `glossary.md`. Use its definition, module and book section as your anchor, so your explanation matches the site and the book.

2. Answer in this shape, short:
   - **One-line definition** in plain English.
   - **An analogy or a concrete example**, ideally from the pre-work (Parcelly's `lookup_order`, the tip splitter, Tidewell's tickets) or from FDE work with a client.
   - **Why it matters in the bootcamp**: one sentence.
   - **Read more:** the book section and the pre-work module, for example "Book §3.2 · M03".

3. If the term isn't in the glossary, answer anyway in the same shape, and say if it's outside the pre-work.

4. End with one quick question that checks understanding, for example "So if the model asks for `lookup_order`, who actually runs it?"
