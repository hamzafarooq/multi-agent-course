## 1. Background

ACME operates fourteen (14) Sentinel-class ground radar installations. The
current signal processing subsystem runs on hardware that reaches end of
manufacturer support in March 2028. This SOW covers modernization of the signal
processing chain and the operator console software across all fourteen sites.

## 2. Scope of work

The Contractor shall:

1. Replace the legacy signal processing unit with the SPX-4 processing stack at
   all fourteen sites.
2. Port the existing operator console application to the modernized interface
   layer, preserving all current operator workflows.
3. Achieve a track-detection accuracy of no less than **98.5%** against the
   agreed reference scenario set, measured at Site 07.
4. Deliver updated technical manuals and conduct operator training at each site.
5. Provide six (6) months of post-deployment sustainment following final site
   acceptance.

Out of scope: antenna hardware, power infrastructure, facility modifications,
and any work at the two Sentinel sites scheduled for decommissioning (Sites 11
and 13).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

A deliverable is accepted when the Client provides written acceptance, or after
twenty (20) business days from submission if the Client has neither accepted nor
rejected it in writing. Rejection must state the specific deficiency. The
Contractor has fifteen (15) business days to correct and resubmit.

Site deployment is accepted only after seventy-two (72) hours of continuous
operation without a Severity 1 or Severity 2 defect.

## 5. Government furnished equipment

the integration lab by 15 July 2026, the reference scenario data set, and

Late delivery of GFE beyond thirty (30) days constitutes an excusable delay and
