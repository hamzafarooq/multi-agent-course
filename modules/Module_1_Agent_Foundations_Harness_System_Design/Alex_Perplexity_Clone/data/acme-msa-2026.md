**Effective date:** 1 March 2026
**Term:** Three (3) years, expiring 28 February 2029
**Parties:** Leidos ("Contractor") and ACME Aerospace Systems ("Client")

## 1. Scope

This Master Services Agreement governs all engineering, integration, and
sustainment services performed by the Contractor for the Client. Individual
efforts are authorized through separately executed Statements of Work (SOWs).
No work is authorized, and no cost is reimbursable, until the applicable SOW is
signed by both parties.

## 2. Contract type and ceiling

The MSA is structured as an Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ)
vehicle. The aggregate ceiling across all SOWs is **$48,500,000** over the
three-year term. The guaranteed minimum is $250,000.

Individual SOWs may be issued as Firm Fixed Price (FFP) or Cost Plus Fixed Fee
(CPFF). Where a SOW is silent on contract type, FFP is presumed.

## 3. Payment terms

- Invoices are submitted monthly, within ten (10) business days of month end.
- Payment is due **Net 45** from receipt of a correct invoice.
- Invoices in dispute must be contested in writing within fifteen (15) business
  days; undisputed portions are paid on the normal schedule.
- A retainage of **five percent (5%)** is withheld on each FFP milestone and
  released upon final acceptance of the associated deliverable.

## 4. Change control

Any change to scope, schedule, or price requires a written Contract Change
Notice (CCN) signed by both parties' authorized representatives. Verbal
direction does not constitute authorization to proceed. The Contractor is not
obligated to perform work outside an executed SOW or CCN, and the Client is not
obligated to pay for it.

The Contractor shall respond to a Client-issued CCN request with a rough order
of magnitude (ROM) estimate within ten (10) business days.

## 6. Intellectual property

Background IP remains the property of the party that brought it. Foreground IP
developed specifically under a SOW and funded entirely by the Client vests in
the Client, subject to a perpetual, royalty-free license back to the Contractor
for internal use. Commercial off-the-shelf components remain governed by their
original licenses.

## 7. Service levels

For sustainment SOWs, the Contractor shall meet the following:

- **Severity 1** (system down, mission impact): response within 1 hour,
  restoration target 8 hours.
- **Severity 2** (degraded capability): response within 4 business hours,
  restoration target 3 business days.
- **Severity 3** (minor defect, workaround exists): response within 2 business
  days, resolved in the next scheduled release.

Sustained failure to meet Severity 1 response times across three consecutive
months entitles the Client to a service credit of 2% of that month's invoice.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with sixty (60) days written notice.
On termination for convenience, the Client pays for work performed through the
termination date plus reasonable wind-down costs, not to exceed 10% of the
remaining SOW value. Termination for cause requires a thirty (30) day cure
period.
