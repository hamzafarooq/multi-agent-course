# Northwind Logistics: Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

## 1. About this policy

1.1 This policy explains how Northwind Logistics pays back money that employees spend on company business.

1.2 In this policy, "manager" means the person you report to, "director" means a director or anyone above a director, and "Finance" means the Finance team in the Chicago office.

1.3 Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own.

1.4 This version replaces the 2024 policy. Claims for money spent before 1 January 2026 follow the 2024 policy.

## 2. Every claim

2.1 Every claim must be submitted within 30 days of the date the money was spent.

2.2 Every claim must state a business purpose.

2.3 A receipt must be attached to any claim of $25 or more.

2.4 Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.

## 3. Meals

3.1 A meal with no client present may cost up to $60 per person.

3.2 A client meal costing over $75 per person must be approved by a director.

3.3 A client meal claim must list every attendee and the company they work for.

3.4 Alcohol is paid back only when a client is present.

## 4. Travel

4.1 Flights must be booked in economy class.

4.2 A business class flight is allowed only when the flight is longer than 6 hours, and only with pre-approval from a vice president.

4.3 Hotels are paid up to $220 a night, or up to $300 a night in New York, San Francisco and London.

4.4 Meals while travelling overnight are paid at the current per-diem rate published by Finance, instead of being claimed one by one.

4.5 Taxis and ride-share trips are paid back. A rental car needs pre-approval from your manager.

## 5. Approvals

5.1 Any claim over $500 must be approved by a director.

5.2 No one may approve their own claim.

5.3 Managers are encouraged to review claims promptly, so that people are paid on time.

## 6. What we don't pay

6.1 We do not pay for fines, parking tickets or personal entertainment.

6.2 Finance may ask for more detail about any claim.

6.3 A claim that breaks this policy may be refused, and repeated breaks are reported to your manager.
